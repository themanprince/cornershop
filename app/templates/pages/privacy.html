This policy explains what information Cornershop ("we", "us") collects when you use our online store, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to the emails we send you about your account and orders.

1. Information we collect

From your Google account

You sign in to Cornershop with Google. With your permission, Google shares the following with us (the openid, email and profile permissions):

  • your name,
  • your email address and whether Google has verified it,
  • a link to your Google profile picture.

We do not ask for, and cannot access, your Google password, Gmail messages, contacts, Drive files, calendar or any other Google data.

Information you give us when you order

  • the name, phone number and delivery address you enter at checkout,
  • the products and quantities you order.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Paystack. You enter your card or bank details on Paystack's checkout page, not on Cornershop. We never receive or store your full card number, CVV or PIN. Paystack tells us the payment reference, the amount, the currency and whether the payment succeeded, and we keep that with your order.

Cookies

We use one cookie, which keeps you signed in and remembers the items in your cart and the delivery details you entered most recently. It is signed so it can't be tampered with. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic request logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the service running and secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To show your name and profile picture while you are signed in.
  • To process, deliver and support your orders, including contacting you about a delivery.
  • To send you service emails: a welcome email when you first sign in, and an order confirmation when a payment succeeds. We do not send marketing emails.
  • To prevent fraud, keep the service secure, and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

3. How we use Google user data

Cornershop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use your Google name, email address and profile picture only to provide the features described in this policy: signing in, your account, and emails about your account and orders.
  • We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not transfer it to others except as needed to run the service (see section 4), to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of the business with notice to you.
  • No person at Cornershop reads your data except with your consent, for security purposes, to comply with the law, or to run the service (for example, an administrator viewing an order to deliver it).
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run Cornershop for us:

  • Google: to sign you in.
  • Paystack: to process payments. Your email address and the amount are sent to start a payment.
  • Brevo: to deliver our emails to you.
  • Supabase: our database and file storage, where your account and order records are kept.
  • Render: our hosting provider, which runs the website.

These providers may process data outside Nigeria. They process it only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers and our business.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account exists. We keep order and payment records for as long as we need them for delivery, customer support, and accounting and tax obligations. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal information except order records we must keep by law. We remove your name, email address and picture where we can.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction and deletion: email us to get a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Revoke Google access: you can remove Cornershop's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You will then need to sign in again to place orders.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under data protection laws such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, including the right to object to processing and to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Security

The site uses HTTPS. Access to our database, storage and payment accounts uses secret keys kept on our servers, never in your browser. Payment details are handled entirely by Paystack. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you and the authorities about a breach where the law requires it.

8. Children

Cornershop is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will tell you by email or on the website before it takes effect.
