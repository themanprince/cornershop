Why shop with us

  • Payments handled by Paystack. Your card details go straight to Paystack. Cornershop never sees or stores them.
  • Clear prices in naira. The price you see is the price you pay, checked against our records again at checkout.
  • Works on your phone. Browse, check out and pay from any device.

About signing in with Google

We use Google sign-in so you don't need another password. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Cornershop. We use them only to:

  • create your Cornershop account and keep you signed in,
  • show your name and picture while you're signed in,
  • send you a welcome email and receipts for your orders.

We never post to your Google account, read your emails or contacts, or sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.
